LOOPET / Your day. Your pace. / Dynamic English promo

16 seconds, 1080 x 1920, 9:16, 60 fps, H.264 MP4 + stereo AAC.
English copy and actual English app screenshots, matching the language of
the preceding promo. Built as a new version; earlier videos are preserved.

0-2s    YOUR DAY. YOUR PACE. / Character landing, rotating rings, kinetic labels.
2-5s    PLAN IT. / Rotating phone entrance and push-in on the real home screen.
5-8s    CHECK IT. / Real progress summary and completed routine cards slide in.
8-10s   GLANCE. GO. / Home screen widget pulled forward for a readable close-up.
10-12s  PICK YOUR SIDEKICK. / Cat, red panda, and sea otter character carousel.
12-16s  MAKE TODAY YOURS. / App icon, LOOPET, and Start your routine CTA.

Short diagonal wipes connect the scenes. A moving footer and small character
accents maintain motion while the final CTA is held for reading.
Music: locally synthesized 120 BPM pluck, bass, kick, clap, hi-hat, and transition
effects. No external music, voiceover, or stock footage was used.

Phone screens and enlarged cards are sourced from the actual Flutter English
captures generated for the previous video. This is a motion-graphics edit of
screenshots and character art, not a live app recording. Progress values and
completed cards are the existing example data; no extra app behavior is invented.
Character artwork comes from approved project assets. Additional character packs
are identified with the line 'Extra character packs available'.

Rebuild from repository root:
python3 design/promo/loopet-dynamic-en/render_dynamic.py

Storyboard only:
python3 design/promo/loopet-dynamic-en/render_dynamic.py --preview
