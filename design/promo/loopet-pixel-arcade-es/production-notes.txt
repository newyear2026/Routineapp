LOOPET / Pixel Arcade / Spanish version

16 seconds, 1080 x 1920, vertical 9:16, 60 fps, H.264 MP4 with stereo AAC.
All promotional copy and the actual in-app screens are Spanish. Motion,
characters, timing, colors, and original 128 BPM music match the English
Pixel Arcade version. The music contains no voiceover.

Spanish copy uses accents, inverted exclamation marks, and the letter ñ.
Pixelify Sans coverage was checked, and longer lines are fitted to the same
safe layout widths. Brand name LOOPET remains unchanged.

The app imagery was freshly captured from actual Flutter widgets using locale
es and character pack cat_starlight. Captures contain example routine data.
This is screenshot-based motion graphics, not a live app recording.
Earlier English and Korean videos are preserved.

python3 design/promo/loopet-pixel-arcade-es/render_arcade_es.py
