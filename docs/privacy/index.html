Privacy Policy

DayRound (하루한바퀴 · Vuelta al Día)
Package: com.dayround.app
Last updated: September 8, 2026

Summary: The developer collects no personal information. There are no user accounts and no servers. Everything you enter — your routines, your history, your settings — stays on your device and is never sent anywhere, including to the ad network. The app is free and shows Google AdMob ads, and to serve them Google processes an advertising identifier. See sections 4 and 5.

1. Information We Collect

The developer collects no personal information. We do not collect or transmit your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, or photos. The app has no account registration or sign-in functionality, and the developer receives no data from the app under any circumstances.

The one exception is advertising. To display ads, Google AdMob processes an advertising identifier (the Android Advertising ID) and coarse device information. That data goes to Google, not to the developer, who sees only aggregate ad performance statistics such as impression counts and revenue. Section 5 covers this in full.

2. Information Stored on Your Device

To provide its features, the app stores the following only in your device's local storage. This information is never transmitted anywhere and is not accessible to the developer.

  • Routines you create (name, time, repeat days, icon and color)
  • Completion records (whether and when a routine was completed)
  • App settings (theme, language, notification and sound preferences)
  • Onboarding progress

Storage uses the standard local mechanisms provided by the operating system: SharedPreferences on Android and UserDefaults on iOS.

3. Home Screen Widget

To display today's routines in the home screen widget, the app writes the minimal summary needed for display to a shared storage area on your device that both the app and the widget can read. On iOS this is an App Group (group.com.dayround.app); on Android it is the app's private storage. This information also never leaves your device.

4. Permissions

  • Notifications — used to remind you at the routine times you set. All notifications are local notifications generated on your device; no push notification server is involved. If you decline this permission, every feature other than notifications continues to work normally.
  • Run at startup and vibration — used incidentally by the notification feature, so that scheduled reminders survive a device restart and can vibrate when they fire.
  • Internet and network state — used only to fetch ads. Your routines and history are never transmitted.
  • Advertising ID (com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID) — used by Google AdMob to serve ads and prevent click fraud.

The app requests no sensitive permission such as camera, microphone, location, contacts, or external storage.

5. Advertising and Third Parties

We do not share, sell, or rent user information to third parties.

To cover its operating costs, the app is free and displays ads from Google AdMob (Google LLC). AdMob may collect and process an advertising identifier and coarse device information — device model, operating system version, approximate region — in order to serve ads and prevent click fraud. That processing is governed by Google's privacy policy.

  • Your routines, completion history, and settings are never sent to the ad network. Ad requests carry none of the data you entered into the app.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings to opt out of personalized ads (Android: Settings › Privacy › Ads).
  • If you are in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, the app asks for your consent through Google's User Messaging Platform before requesting any ad. Without consent, no personalized ads are requested.

Apart from the Google Mobile Ads SDK, the app contains no third-party SDKs — no analytics tools and no crash reporting tools.

6. Children's Privacy

The app is not directed at children under 13, and the developer collects no personal information from users of any age. The app is not enrolled in a families programme, and ad requests are not tagged as child-directed.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

All data is kept solely on your device for as long as you use the app. You may delete individual routines within the app, and uninstalling the app removes all stored data from your device. Because the developer holds no user data, there is no separate deletion request process to follow.

8. Data Security

None of the data you enter leaves your device, so there is no risk of it being intercepted in transit. The only network traffic the app generates is ad requests to Google, sent over HTTPS and carrying none of your routine data. Data stored on the device is protected from other applications by the operating system's application sandbox.

9. Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, we will post the revised policy and its effective date on this page. Significant changes will also be noted in the app's update description on the store listing.
